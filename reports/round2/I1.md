Author: improvement engineer I1. Every number here comes from code run in this work package (`work/I1_clip/`,
`uwbfall/clip.py`). F1 values use the official `abc2027.evaluate` code with `--ignore-flagged`:
- SR-CV is scored through `srcv.scoring`.
- Val is scored through the official CLI.
- Promotion decisions use `srcv.promote` through the R2_EVAL tooling: paired subject-cluster bootstrap with 5000 resamples, P(Δ>0) ≥ 0.90, at least 3/4 folds improved, and val no worse than −0.02.

Every comparison averages **3 TCN bags** (`uwb_default`, `tcn_bag1`, `tcn_bag2`; the HGB is deterministic). The

- REF → G7N4: Δ **+0.028** [95% CI +0.012, +0.045], **P = 1.000**, **4/4** folds improve. Val drops by 0.009, within the rule's 0.02 tolerance. **PASS.**
- Each step also passes when added on its own:
  - REF → N4 (time NMS): Δ +0.014, P = 1.000, 4/4 folds, val unchanged.
  - N4 → G7N4 (gate on top of NMS): Δ +0.014, P = 0.987, 3/4 folds, val −0.009.
  - G7 → G7N4 (NMS on top of the gate): Δ +0.015, P = 1.000, 4/4 folds.
- Mechanism, from the @0.3 error anatomy over 3 bags:
  - **NMS** removes almost all duplicate fragments inside matched fall clips, the pre-fall, post-fall and overlapping extra rows: 8 / 11 / 10 per bag → 1 / 1 / 1.
  - **The gate** halves the FPs inside ADL pieces: 18 → 12, 20 → 10 and 10 → 6 per bag. Through the lower tuned threshold it also recovers a few FNs that had no prediction (9 → 7 and 11 → 7 in 2 of 3 bags).
  - Pooled FPs @0.3 fall from 35.7 to 20.0 and FA/h from 4.33 to 2.43. Recall is unchanged (TP 238.0 → 238.7).
  - What does not improve: mislocalised rows in fall clips whose GT stays unmatched (7 / 9 / 6 → 6 / 10 / 9), with the matching FNs "with a prediction nearby". These are boundary or detection-placement errors, not clip-level ones.
- **Not promoted:**
  - Segment NMS ("at most one event per junction-to-junction segment", NS) beats REF (P = 0.982, 4/4), but it loses to plain time NMS: N4 → NS P = 0.06, and G7N4 → G7NS P = 0.08, 0/4 folds. It costs about 3 TPs (FN @0.3 16.0 → 19.3). This fits rows being suppressed across a missed junction, though that mechanism was not traced event by event.
  - The **3-class post-fall HGB** (D) fails: REF → D P = 0.16, and N4 → DN4 P = 0.31, 2/4 folds, val −0.019.
  - The gate with floor 0 is too harsh: on bag 0, val falls to 0.921 and 1/4 folds improve.
- **Caveat on val:** val drops in all 3 bags (−0.002 / −0.012 / −0.012; mean −0.009). The cause is about one event per bag out of 67: an extra post-fall FP or a wider replay interval (§4). SR-CV has 253 events and agrees across all 3 bags and 4 folds, so under the rule the change is kept. The lead should still know that on official val the new reference is 0.952, not 0.965.

## 1. What was built (all behind config flags in `uwbfall`)

| flag | piece | details |
|---|---|---|
| `clip.junction_*` | (a) variance-normalised junction detector | `clip.junction_stat` computes s(i) = log10 ‖m_after − m_before‖² / (v_before + v_after) on the 0.2 s complex static profile of the 3 radars, with W = 5-block windows on each side. Peaks above a threshold are kept with a minimum separation of 1 s. The threshold is the 5th percentile of the statistic at true fall-clip junctions (±2 blocks) on the **training** streams, so recall of fall junctions is about 0.95. |
| `clip.segclf.*` | (b) LightGBM segment classifier, classes adl / fall clip / autre-empty | Applied to junction-to-junction segments. The 18 features cover static change across the segment (per radar and max), motion burst (static5/static10/diff max, p90 and mean inside the segment), sample-and-hold repeat shares (rate, spacing-2/3/4 share and count, **computed within the stream only**), segment length and the two junction strengths. The PCA-fingerprint segment means (`fpm*`) are implemented, but feature set "nofp" excludes them (chosen on train data, §2). Labels come from the training composition. The OOF P(fall clip) on the training streams uses **GroupKFold by segment subject**, so that the decode tuning does not see leaky gates. |
| `clip.gate`, `clip.gate_floor` | (c1) gate frame posteriors by P(fall clip) | p ← p · (floor + (1 − floor) · P(fall clip of the block's segment)). The operating point is re-tuned on the gated train OOF. |
| `decode.one_event_per_clip`, `nms_gap_s`, `nms_max_span_s` | (c2) hard 1-D NMS on the final, replayed rows | `"time"`: greedy by score, drop a row whose interval lies within `gap` s of a kept row. `"segment"`: at most one row per junction-to-junction segment. Junctions inside a row (±0.4 s) are ignored, because the fall's own static change is a jump. Rows share a segment only if their span is ≤ 18.7 s, the generator's longest fall clip. |
| `hgb.postfall_class` | (d) post-fall negative class | 3-class HGB on the prototype 64 features: other / fall / post-fall-in-clip. Post-fall means a block inside the fall clip that holds the event, later than t_end + 0.5 s. P(fall) replaces the HGB member in the blend. |
| — | `pipeline.retune` / `python -m uwbfall retune` | Re-runs the post-model stages of `fit` on an existing bundle, without refitting HGB or TCN: clip stage, gated OOF → operating point, OOF replay, NMS. Old bundles can then be upgraded to the new reference in about 3 minutes. |

Nothing uses another stream at prediction time. The cross-stream ADL duplicate mask stays OFF.

Time NMS does not change the val score. The N4 val CSV is byte-identical to REF in 2 bags; in tcn_bag2 it removes
two low-score rows (0.57 and 0.59), with identical counts at every θ. The gate re-tunes the operating point (bag 0:
(0.5, 5, 0.8, 0.2) → (0.4, 5, 0.8, 0.2)), and that shifts detector bounds. The replay crops then move, and about
10 val rows per bag get slightly different bounds. The two recurring losses are:
- a post-fall row at val_02 ≈ 314.0–315.6 s, 2 s after the GT fall 309.9–311.9, with score 0.45–0.49, present in all 3 bags;
- val_03 1493.4–1497.0 against GT 1494.2–1495.0. The new crop gives a wider interval, so tIoU is 0.22 and the match is lost at θ ≥ 0.3.

These are about one event per bag out of 67, inside the ±0.04 val noise band. SR-CV gains are consistent across
bags (sd 0.003) and folds.

## 6. Cost and how to ship

- **Fit cost:** the clip stage adds about 1 min to `fit` with a warm feature cache, and about 2.5 min cold: junction statistics, segment tables, and 5 LightGBM fits on about 1.6k segments. Predict adds about 1–2 s per stream. `retune` took 7.5 min on the 13 h5 train streams. Most of that is the OOF replay (5 min), measured with a cold page cache and a concurrent job. On regenerated virtual streams it took 10.5 min.
- **To ship the new reference from an existing bundle without retraining:**
  `python3 -m uwbfall retune --models <bundle> --train-splits <its splits> --out <dir>`. The default `--config` is `configs/reference.json`.
- **For the final train+val fit:** `python3 -m uwbfall train --train-splits train,val` now includes the clip stage by default. It needs the composition rows of the training streams, which are package training labels (`streams_composition.csv`, or the in-memory plan of virtual streams).
- Compute in this work package: about 15 min for prep (2 lanes), about 45 min for decode variants (2 lanes), about 10 min for scoring, and about 10 min for parity. No TCN was retrained.

## 7. Limitations and open issues

- The val drop (0.9606 → 0.9517, 3-bag mean; bag 0 0.9646 → 0.9524) is within the rule but real in all 3 bags. If the lead weights the 5 official val streams more than the rule does, N4 alone (val unchanged, SR-CV +0.014, P = 1.000, 4/4) is the conservative fallback: `configs/C3G3.json` plus `{"decode": {"one_event_per_clip": "time", "nms_gap_s": 4}}`.
- The same 20 SR-CV test streams were used for all R2 decisions, including the R2_EVAL gate 0.3 and these 10+ variants. Gate floor and NMS gap were chosen on train OOF, but the set of variants scored on SR-CV invites mild selection. A fresh SR-CV regeneration would be the clean confirmation.
- The segment classifier is trained on the training composition with segments from the *detected* junctions. A missed junction merges a fall clip with its neighbour, and recall of fall junctions is about 0.93–0.98 on held-out streams. Merged segments explain why segment NMS loses to time NMS.
- The 3-class post-fall HGB was tested only as a replacement of the HGB member. A post-fall class inside the TCN (X3 P0-4, full form) was not tried; it would need TCN retraining with 3 bags, about 1.7 h.
- Gate form, floor and the NMS gap are frozen for the train+val fit. They are not re-selected per fit.
