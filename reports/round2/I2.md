# I2: Chute04 boundaries, D3 hedge, ignore-zone loss masking (PLAN v2 W4; experts X1 / X3)

Author: improvement engineer I2. Every number here comes from code run in this work package (`work/I2_boundaries/`,
see its README). F1 values use the official `abc2027.evaluate` code with `--ignore-flagged`:
- SR-CV is scored through `srcv.scoring`, pooled over 20 regenerated test streams (253 evaluable falls).
- Val is scored through the official CLI.
- Promotion decisions use `srcv.promote` through the R2_EVAL tooling: a paired subject-cluster bootstrap with 5000 resamples, averaged over 3 TCN bags. A change passes only with P(Δ>0) ≥ 0.90, at least 3/4 folds improved, and val no worse than −0.02.

The baseline is the **current reference G7N4** (I1). Every variant starts from it and changes one thing. It re-tunes its
operating point on its own G7-gated training OOF (official evaluator, uwbfall grid, first max), then runs the
reference replay (gate 0.3) and time NMS at 4 s. With no change, the I2 tooling rebuilds the reference
**byte-for-byte** in all 3 bags × 5 targets (`decvar.py --check`, `dump.py`).

Main findings:
1. **The Chute04 problem has moved.** Under the reference (gate-0.3 replay), the boundaries of *detected* Chute04
   falls are about as good as for the other types: tIoU ≥ 0.5 for 0.897 of them, against 0.905 for Chute02. The val
   finding N9 (Chute04 hit@0.5 = 0.43) was measured with the prototype's detector bounds; with detector bounds Chute04 reaches only
   0.760 on SR-CV too. Chute04's remaining deficit is **detection**. 6.3 of its 55 evaluable falls per bag (11.5 %)
   have no candidate at all, against 0.7–1.0 of 56–71 for each other type. That accounts for 56 % of Chute04's failures at θ = 0.5.
   No boundary rule can recover these.
2. Boundary headroom exists, but none of the three fixes captured it. At θ = 0.5, picking the best of
   {median, each crop, detector} per candidate (an oracle) would raise the hit rate from 0.901 to 0.972 (Chute04 0.78 → 0.91).
   - The largest-cluster rule is worse in every fold, and on training data it was already at or below the median in 13/15 cells.
   - The learned selector is a wash: Chute04 hit@0.5/0.7 rises from .794/.612 to .806/.655, but other types lose as much.
     It has only about 185 training candidates with unflagged GT per (bag, target).
3. **D3 hedge.** Test_P3_3, the only evaluable D3 subject (16 falls, SR-CV fold 3), already has recall **0.958**
   (15.3/16 per bag at θ 0.1/0.3/0.5) under the reference. Zone features in the HGB lift it to 0.979 (Z) or leave it
   at 0.958 (Z0), but both cost 0.008–0.011 pooled F1 on the other subjects.
   - The one recurring P3_3 miss (cv3_test_03 at 784.7 s, missed in 2 of 3 bags) has a strong TCN peak (0.78–0.85) and a reference HGB of 0.00.
   - The I1 segment gate gives it P(fall clip) = 0.31, which lowers the blended peak to 0.46–0.51, near the 0.4–0.5 thresholds.
   - It is then lost in decoding: below threshold in bag 2, and by smoothing or min-duration in bag 0.
4. **Loss masking** of the 18–26 ignore-zone fall clips per training set (≈ 1.2 % of blocks) hurts the HGB member
   (TP @0.3 238.7 → 235.7). In the TCN, a 1-bag screen shows the intended mechanism. With bag 0, FPs @0.3 fall from 21 to 15 (TW) or 19 (TWt), and Test_P3_3 goes from 15/16 to 16/16 in both. But 3–5 TPs are lost, val falls by 0.005, and P is 0.63–0.70 with wide CIs. That is inconclusive, and a 3-bag test (10 more TCN fits, about 2.2 h) did not fit the time box.
5. The null control shows that the reference's operating point is **not** fragile. A 3 % change of the HGB gives
   Δ −0.0001 with a CI of ±0.003. The −0.008 to −0.011 losses of Z, Z0 and W are therefore real effects, not
   re-tuning noise or a winner's curse on the reference. The same control moves val by −0.007 in one bag
   (0.952 → 0.945), which shows the val noise floor directly.

## 2. (a) Boundary changes

All three keep every boundary on an annotator output (`propose_fall` on a crop) or on the detector bounds. None
learns a shift or pad, so the start-error tie-break is protected: the median |start error| @0.3 stays 0.040 s in every variant.

- **CL8, mode-aware aggregation** (`boundary.agg="cluster"`, `cluster_iou` 0.8). R2_EVAL named this as the next experiment.
  Among the crops that pass the gate, keep the largest group within tIoU 0.8 of a medoid crop, then take the median.
  - Train-only diagnostic (`aggdiag.py`, train-OOF candidates vs unflagged train GT, mean hit over θ .1/.3/.5): the median is at or above every cluster threshold in 13/15 (target, bag) cells.
  - SR-CV agrees: Δ −0.0068, 0/4 folds. The loss is at θ = 0.5 (F1 .887 → .869).
  - When the 6 crops split 3 vs 3, the midpoint of the two modes evidently overlaps the true fall more often than either mode alone.
- **fc, containment fallback** (`boundary.fallback="contain"`). Applies only when no crop passes the gate (3.3 failures per bag).
  It uses the crops lying mostly inside the detector interval. On training OOF with frac 0.8 it is better in 2 cells, worse in 4 and equal in 9, for a summed Δ of −0.013.
  frac 0.6 and 1.0 are no better. **Rejected on train data; not scored on SR-CV,** to avoid spending the test streams.
- **S, boundary-hypothesis selector** (`boundary.hypotheses / fit_selector / select`).
  - Each candidate gets hypotheses {median, each of the 6 crops, detector}, all with the 1 s duration floor.
  - 22 features: crop agreement and support, start/end spread, the crop's anchor radar, `quality == low`, the number of review reasons, onset spread, offsets to the detector and to junctions, and the detector score.
  - A LightGBM regressor predicts each hypothesis's tIoU with the GT, trained on the bag's train-OOF candidates of the target. Targets use unflagged training GT only.
  - The switch margin {0, .02, .05, .1, never} is chosen by a stream-grouped inner CV on train. "Never" won in 6 of 15 cells, and the inner-CV gain was ≤ 0.007 elsewhere.
  - SR-CV: Δ −0.0002, P = 0.47, 2/4 folds.
  - Chute04 hit@0.5/0.7 improves (.794 → .806, .612 → .655), but the selector's mistakes on other types cancel it.
  - The training set (about 185 candidates with GT per bag and target) is too small for 7-way selection. It is kept as library code, but fit/predict refuse the flag.

## 3. (b) D3 hedge

The zone features (`features.zones`, `features.zone_features`) go into the **HGB member only**. The TCN already has range-band
channels covering bins 40–53 and 53–69 on every radar, so its 112-channel input is unchanged and no TCN refit is needed.
For each zone [radar, a, b) the HGB gets:
- static change over 5 and 10 blocks of the zone's complex profile, normalised by the zone's median norm;
- zone motion (mot_map);
- the zone frame-jump;
- the max over zones of the 10-block change shifted by ±1 s and ±2 s.

Zones: Z = R29171 [40, 69), R29444 [5, 22) and R30722 [5, 22), 16 features. Z0 = R29171 [40, 69) only, 10 features.
All features are per stream, with no cross-stream information.

## 4. (c) Ignore-zone loss masking

`labels.ignore_clips = "zero_weight"` gives zero loss weight to every block of a fall clip that is a generator ignore zone (`features.ignore_clip_mask`).
- Ignore zone means the clip holds a flagged fall (quality low or needs_review) whose onset is < 0.25 s after the clip start (a junction-start fall), or the clip begins with a hard cut (zero crossfade, or the stream start).
- Flagged falls elsewhere stay positives, and unflagged hard-cut falls are never masked.
- In the package's train+val composition (478 events), the definition selects the 24 junction-start clips with hard cuts, the 3 junction-start clips with crossfade, and 2 flagged hard-cut clips whose fall starts later. 2 unflagged hard-cut clips stay unmasked.
- Per training target: 23 / 26 / 19 / 18 / 23 clips (folds 0–3, official), i.e. 962–1435 blocks (≈ 1.2 %).
- HGB rows with weight 0 are dropped. The TCN uses a weighted BCE, sum(w·bce)/sum(w).

- **HGB arm (W, 3 bags):** Δ −0.0076, P = 0.008, 0/4 folds, val −0.002. TP @0.3 falls from 238.7 to 235.7. Removing the
  junction-start positives costs recall without reducing FPs (20.0 → 21.7).
- **TCN arm (1-bag screen):** `tcnw.py` fitted a TCN-only bag with the masked loss and the bag-0 (E4) seeds on all 4 SR-CV
  fold-trains and the 13 official train streams. That is 5 fits of about 26 min each, bundles in `/home/user/data_raw/I2/runs/tcnW_bag0`. Each fit

## 7. Limitations and open issues

- **TCN arm of (c):** only a 1-bag screen was run, and it is positive but inconclusive (P 0.63–0.70; −5 FP, −3 to −5 TP; val −0.005). If the lead wants the full test, these would complete it:
  - 2 more bags: `python3 work/I2_boundaries/tcnw.py tcnW_bag1 10000 0,1,2,3,official` and `... tcnW_bag2 20000 ...`, about 2.2 h on 2 lanes. The seed offsets match R2_EVAL's tcn_bag1/2.
  - Then, per bag, `decvar.py 0,1,2,3,official TWt --tcn tcnW_bagK --bags <REF bag with the same seeds>`. With `--tcn`, the
    TCN member is replaced, and `--bags` only names the output directory and the REF bag it is compared with.
  - Then `score.py promote REF TWt`.
- The boundary selector is limited by its training data (about 185 candidates with unflagged GT per bag and target). The oracle headroom is large (θ 0.5: 0.901 → 0.972), so a version trained on more data might work. More data could come from replaying jittered pseudo-candidates around every training GT, which would multiply the training set by about 10 at roughly 5 min of replay per target. That was not done here.
- **Chute04 is now a detection-recall problem** (6.3/55 missed per bag). The next experiments belong to W3/W5, not W4:
  - a lower operating threshold or min-duration inside high-P(fall clip) segments;
  - a Chute04-aware event model;
  - checking the segment gate on short or late falls (2 of the misses are gate attenuations).
- The D3 hedge was tested only in the HGB member. P3_3 is already at 0.958, and its one recurring miss is gate-attenuated with an HGB of 0.00, so any further D3 work should target the segment classifier. Test_P3_3 is one subject with 16 falls; the D3 evidence rests on it.
- All I2 variants were scored on the same 20 SR-CV test streams as R2_EVAL and I1. Train-only checks rejected fc before it was scored, and all other variants failed, so selection bias does not change any decision here.
- Compute: about 1 h 45 min wall (16:33–18:15) on up to 2 lanes. Replay dumps took about 15 min; HGB refits about 2.5 min per variant and target; the 5 TCN-W fits about 26 min each. Nothing was committed. Caches are in `work/I2_boundaries/cache` (git-ignored); TCN-W bundles are in `/home/user/data_raw/I2/runs/tcnW_bag0`.
