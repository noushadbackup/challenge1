# Round 3: improvement experiments and ablation (2026-10-16)

Base: `final_norep` (the submission). Every candidate is judged on SR-CV with the promotion rule:
- paired subject-cluster bootstrap P(gain) >= 0.90;
- at least 3 of 4 folds improve;
- val no more than 0.02 below the base.

Each experiment was run by its own agent. A second, independent agent then re-scored it from the CSVs and checked
for leakage and rule compliance.

| Experiment | Idea | Best result (SR-CV, vs base) | Rule | Verifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| recover (`work/R3_recover`) | Add rows for missed falls in likely fall clips | +0.0003, P 0.54, 1/4 folds | fail | confirmed |
| boundary (`work/R3_boundary`) | Blend replay bounds with detector bounds (`wdet_cv`); TriDet-style boundary head | wdet_cv +0.0083, P 0.995, 3/4 folds; 3 seed sets +0.0057, P 0.989; boundary head negative | **pass** | confirmed |
| verify (`work/R3_verify`) | Range-time images + ImageNet ResNet-18 verifier (open-source weights) | +0.0126, P 0.873, 1/4 folds only | fail | confirmed |

| ablation (`ABLATION.md`) | Remove one component at a time, 3 seed sets | removing range bands +0.009, P 0.99, 3/4 folds | **pass** | team PC run |

## What we learned

- **Missed falls.** 8 of 14 have a row in the right clip but in the wrong place, so adding rows cannot fix them.
  Chute04 (fall from a chair) misses come from the segment classifier: its static change and motion burst are weaker.
- **Boundaries.** Most tIoU-0.5 errors happen where the 6 replay crops *agree*. In 33 of 52 such cases the detector
  bounds were already right. Blending the two works; a learned boundary head (about 190 falls per fold) does not
  beat re-running the package annotator.
- **Image verifier.** It separates easy negatives (AUC 0.93–0.96) but not the false alarms that survive the pipeline
  (AUC 0.57–0.70). Its gain is confined to one fold.
- **Ablation.** TCN, NMS and replay are essential. The 48 range-band channels do not help on SR-CV. Fingerprint and
  structure features are neutral within noise.

## Integration and confirmation

- `boundary.det_blend` (optional config key, default off) is now in `uwbfall`.
  - (ws, we) is chosen on the bundle's own training OOF and stored in `meta.boundary_blend`.
  - It reproduces R3's `wdet_cv` byte for byte on all 4 folds and on val, with the same weights.
  - Existing configs and shipped bundles are unchanged (same config hashes, `make checks` and `verify-bundle` OK).
- The two passing changes were found among about 25 tried ideas, so before any switch they are re-tested on
  **fresh TCN seeds** that no selection used: `make confirm DEVICE=cuda` (`tools/confirm.py`, seed sets 3–5).
  - Variants: no range bands, boundary blend, and both, each compared with `final_norep` on the same seeds.
- **Decision rule:** switch the submission only if a candidate passes the promotion rule on the fresh seeds.
  - Cost of the blend: the median start error (tie-break 1) rises from about 0.04 s to about 0.19 s. Prefer the band
    removal alone if both pass with similar gains.

## Outcome of the confirmation (2026-10-17, `CONFIRM.md`)

Neither candidate passes on fresh seeds:
- no range bands: −0.011, P 0.23;
- boundary blend: +0.006, P 0.97, but only 2/4 folds;
- both together: −0.008.

**The submission stays `final_norep`, unchanged.**
